# 09 — Engagement, Communication & HR Helpdesk

## 1. Announcements & news [V1]

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| ENG-001 | Posts with rich text/images/attachments, audience targeting (entity, location, department, grade), scheduling, pinning, mandatory acknowledgement, read analytics. |
| ENG-002 | AI writing assist for posts (draft, shorten, translate). |
| ENG-003 | Celebrations feed: birthdays, work anniversaries, new joiners, promotions (each opt-out per employee for privacy). |

## 2. Surveys & pulse [V2]

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| ENG-010 | Survey builder (question types, logic, anonymity, audience, schedule, reminders); templates (eNPS, engagement, onboarding, exit). |
| ENG-011 | Anonymity guarantee: results only for groups ≥ N respondents; no raw answers to managers for anonymous surveys. |
| ENG-012 | AI analysis of open-text answers: themes, sentiment, representative (anonymized) quotes, suggested actions. |
| ENG-013 | Action planning from results with owners and follow-up. |

## 3. Recognition & rewards [V2]

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| ENG-020 | Peer recognition (kudos) tied to company values, public wall, reactions/comments. |
| ENG-021 | Points/budget-based rewards with redemption catalog [V3]. |
| ENG-022 | Awards programs (employee of the month) with nominations and voting. |

## 4. Events & social [V2]

## 5. HR Helpdesk [V1 basic, V2 full]

| ID | Requirement | Ver |
|---|---|---|
| HD-001 | Employees raise tickets (category, priority, attachment, confidential flag); first try copilot answer with policy citations — "Did this answer your question?" deflection. | V1 |
| HD-002 | Categories routed to teams (HR ops, payroll, IT, admin) with SLAs, assignment rules, internal notes, canned responses. | V1 |
| HD-003 | AI triage: category, priority, suggested owner, draft reply from knowledge base; agent edits and sends. | V1 |

| HD-006 | Case management for sensitive matters (grievances) linked to DSC module. | V2 |
